At each simulation step (4 ticks per minute), the engine will:

Determine Possession State: Based on Midfield ratings.
Event Roll: Generate a random number [0..1] and map it to the Matrix above.
Tactical Scaling: If a GOAL is rolled, the engine compares Home Attack vs Away Defense. If the defender wins, the event is downgraded to a SAVE, CORNER, or TACKLE.
Action Buffer: Every minute will now strive to have 4 clear actions represented (as you requested), ensuring the narration bar never goes quiet.

3. Phase B: The "Visual Executor" (The Pitch)
The 2D Tactical Pitch will no longer try to "invent" movement. It will:

Receive the command: EXECUTE GOAL for HOME.
Move the ball linearly to Tier 4 (Attack), then to the Net.
Trigger the "GOAL" animation.
Receive the command: EXECUTE CUT for AWAY.
Move the ball to MID Tier, then reset it to a defender.

4. Phase C: Tactical Connection
Offensive Style: Increases the probability of GOAL/MISS/SAVE rolls by 20%.
Defensive Style: Increases the probability of TACKLE/FOUL rolls by 20%.
High Pressure: Increases the probability of TACKLE but also FOUL and YELLOW CARD.
